How It Works

How a remodel works

Four phases, one team.

1. Consultation

We meet to talk through the scope of work, your goals and any design preferences.

2. Design

3D room scans, detailed floor plans with layout options and revisions so your layout matches your vision.

3. Refine

Walkthroughs with trade partners and design meetings to finalize materials and make pricing accurate.

4. Production

Skilled craftsmen led by a dedicated lead carpenter bring it to life.
